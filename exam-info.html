AP Physics 1 Exam Info

A quick reference for the exam's science practices, course topics, format, and free-response questions. This is a study aid summary, not an official College Board publication — always check the official AP Physics 1 Course and Exam Description for authoritative details.

Science Practices

The AP Physics 1 exam assesses students on a set of science practices in addition to content knowledge:

  • 1. Modeling: Represent physical phenomena using diagrams, graphs, and models.
  • 2. Mathematical Routines: Select and apply mathematical relationships to solve problems.
  • 3. Scientific Questioning: Pose and refine scientific questions and hypotheses.
  • 4. Experimental Methods: Design and describe experimental procedures.
  • 5. Data Analysis: Analyze and interpret quantitative and qualitative data.
  • 6. Argumentation: Develop and justify scientific arguments using evidence and reasoning.

Exam Format

Section Question Type # of Questions Exam Weighting Timing
I Multiple Choice (including some multi-select) 50 50% 90 minutes
II Free Response 4 50% 90 minutes

Total exam time is 3 hours. A four-function, scientific, or graphing calculator is permitted throughout the entire exam.

Free Response Questions

The free-response section (Section II) typically includes four questions:

  • Experimental Design — design or evaluate an experiment.
  • Qualitative/Quantitative Translation — connect qualitative reasoning with quantitative reasoning.
  • Short Answer (paragraph argument) — explain reasoning in written form.
  • Short Answer — a shorter, more targeted problem.

Full and partial credit is awarded for showing work, using correct reasoning, and applying the science practices above — not just for a final numeric answer.
